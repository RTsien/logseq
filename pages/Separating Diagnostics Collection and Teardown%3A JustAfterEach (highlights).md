	- Moreover, the *nature* of the code being built - complex distributed systems - required a testing framework that could provide for the needs unique to unit-testing and integration-testing such a system. We needed to make testing [asynchronous behavior](https://onsi.github.io/gomega/#making-asynchronous-assertions) ubiquitous and straightforward. We needed to have [parallelizable integration tests](https://onsi.github.io/ginkgo#spec-parallelization) to ensure our test run-times didn't get out of control. We needed a test framework that helped us [suss out](https://onsi.github.io/ginkgo#spec-randomization) flaky tests and fix them.
