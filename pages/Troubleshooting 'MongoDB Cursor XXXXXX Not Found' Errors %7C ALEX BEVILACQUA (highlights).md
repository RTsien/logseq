	- Starting with MongoDB 4.4.8 (via [SERVER-6036](https://jira.mongodb.org/browse/SERVER-6036)) when a cursor is opened as part of a session, its lifetime will be tied to that session and as a result closing or timing out of a session will kill all associated cursors.
