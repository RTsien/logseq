summary:: Greg Wheeler compares using beard balm to taking notes in a Zettelkasten system, emphasizing the importance of quality ideas and connections. He suggests starting small by capturing single thoughts and allowing them to spread throughout your idea network. Writing about connections deepens understanding and transforms the way you think, fostering creativity.
