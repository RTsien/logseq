summary:: Greg Wheeler reflects on the overwhelming number of note-taking apps and the need for a simpler, more intentional approach. He emphasizes taking time to notice quiet ideas, simplifying his note-taking process, and prioritizing deep thinking over endless tinkering. By walking and observing the world around him, he finds new insights and focuses on meaningful note-taking rather than just collecting information.
