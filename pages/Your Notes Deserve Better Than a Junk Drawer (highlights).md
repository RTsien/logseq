summary:: Greg Wheeler is rethinking how to organize his notes by creating what he calls an Idea Mansion, where ideas can connect and grow. He uses a map with different areas like a front porch for raw ideas and rooms for individual notes. Wheeler is considering sharing this concept in a video masterclass.
![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/d14fbcb0-883f-4b8e-b780-db38bdc9cc46_1280x1106.png)

- Highlights first synced by [[Readwise]] [[May 22nd, 2025]]
	- **The Idea Mansion Map:**
	  
	  •   **🌱 Front Porch** – raw ideas and curiosities that haven’t found their place yet
	    
	  •   **🏛️ Wings** – major themes in your life (creativity, faith, design, etc.)
	    
	  •   **🚪 Rooms** – individual notes centered on meaningful questions
	    
	  •   **❓ Doors** – your note titles, phrased as questions that invite you in
	    
	  •   **↔️ Hallways** – links between rooms, forming a web of thought
	    
	  •   **🍳 Kitchen** – your creative space, where projects and drafts are cooking ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jvsv395ns3j7q4r5ckd3dabq))
