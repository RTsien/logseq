- Highlights first synced by [[Readwise]] [[Nov 24th, 2023]]
	- First, hands-free TTS listening is particularly well-suited for consuming low information density content. If you've got a bunch of ~5 minute news articles and press releases sitting in your queue, you might try listening to them on your next walk, drive, bike ride, dishwashing, et cetera. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg0h3f66jhahds3mtn43nwe0))
		- **Note**: 首先，免持式TTS听书非常适合阅读低信息密度的内容。如果你有一堆大约5分钟的新闻文章和新闻稿件放在队列里，你可以尝试在下一次步行、驾车、骑自行车、洗碗等活动中听它们。
	- Second, our implementation of TTS includes accurate word mapping enabling a form of reading sometimes referred to as immersion reading. This refers to simultaneously reading and listening to the same document. It's a kind of sensory deprivation technique enabling you to quickly ramp into a flow state which would otherwise take 15 to 20 minutes of focused reading to attain. This is clearly anecdotal, but you might give it a shot to see how you like it. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hg0h22qajd7f3nw5jw86myb0))
		- **Note**: 第二，我们的TTS实现包括准确的单词映射，可以实现一种有时被称为沉浸式阅读的阅读形式。这指的是同时阅读和听取同一份文档。这是一种感官剥夺技术，使您能够快速进入流动状态，否则需要15到20分钟的专注阅读才能达到。这显然是个人经验，但您可以试试看你是否喜欢。
