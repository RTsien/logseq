summary:: Greg Wheeler reflects on how onion powder, which contains only onion, lasts longer than fresh onions due to its simplicity. He argues that in our digital note-taking, we often believe adding more features improves our notes, but this can actually hinder deep thinking. Instead, reducing complexity can enhance clarity and longevity in our ideas.
