summary:: Language models like Claude learn from vast amounts of data rather than being directly programmed by humans. Research shows that Claude can think ahead and plan its responses while also sometimes creating plausible-sounding arguments that may not be logically sound. Understanding Claude's internal reasoning processes could help improve AI reliability and transparency.
