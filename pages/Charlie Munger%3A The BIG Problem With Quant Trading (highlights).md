summary:: Charlie Munger discusses the challenges of quantitative trading, noting that while short-term algorithms can be successful, they struggle with long-term predictions. He emphasizes that Berkshire Hathaway does not plan to hire a quant to trade stocks, as they don't believe they can predict market movements effectively. Ultimately, Munger trusts their own investment approach over relying on quant strategies.
