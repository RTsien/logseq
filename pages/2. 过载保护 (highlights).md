summary:: The text discusses overload protection in internet systems, focusing on analyzing the root causes and effects of overload, such as performance bottlenecks and system capacity issues. It emphasizes the importance of understanding hardware limitations, managing request queues effectively, and guiding user behavior to prevent system failures under high pressure. The core task of overload protection is to ensure service continuity under pressure, emphasizing the need to identify valid requests, manage workload effectively, and reject unnecessary tasks promptly.

	- 过载保护的核心任务不是提高系统性能，而是保证在高压下服务能力不要陡降到0  
	  因此，从几个方向出发去做过载保护（有背书）
	  
	  1)    了解OS、硬件的性能瓶颈，合理设计系统容量，不去触碰这些瓶颈。 对于内部系统适用。但是暴露给用户的前端无法做到
	  
	  2)    需要能够识别哪些是有效请求，哪些是过期请求。过载时直接清空雪球队列。 防止系统做无用功
	  
	  3)     勇于拒绝、及早拒绝
	  
	  4)     对于用户的重试行为要适当延缓 ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01hv1rgd8neh9q3g1fnamdgy1s))
