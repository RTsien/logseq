document_note:: To learn effectively, focus and alertness are necessary. During periods of deep rest, the brain rewires and replays the same cells that were active during learning, leading to repetition while sleeping. Ultradian rhythms show that focusing for 90-minute cycles can be beneficial, and shallow naps or non-sleep deep rests done within four hours of learning can accelerate learning. Lastly, incremental learning, breaking down learning into brief periods of intense focus, can lead to an outsized amount of learning overall, and the nervous system loves to batch things into focused little bouts.
