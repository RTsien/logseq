	- The big idea is: **if your mind is a mansion, your notes are what fill it with treasure**.
	  
	  But here’s the part I didn’t see coming.
	  
	  After I finished it, I realized it mirrors a verse I’ve loved for years:
	  
	  > “By wisdom a house is built,  
	  > and by understanding it is established;  
	  > by knowledge the rooms are filled  
	  > with all precious and pleasant riches.”  
	  > — *Proverbs 24:3–4*
	  
	  Now — I know this passage isn’t *technically* about note-making or creative thinking.  
	  It’s about building a life, a household, maybe even a legacy.
	  
	  But still… the overlap stopped me in my tracks. ([View Highlight](https://read.readwise.io/read/01jvsskv6j8676y3q6xq3c1gfe))
